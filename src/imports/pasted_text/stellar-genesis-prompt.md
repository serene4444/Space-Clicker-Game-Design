# Figma Prompt — Mature Space Clicker Game

Design a high-fidelity interface and interactive prototype for a mature, atmospheric space-themed incremental clicker game called **Stellar Genesis**.

The game follows the full evolution of a solar system. The player begins as an observer aboard a futuristic station, enters a stylized cosmic environment, creates and matures planets, introduces life, develops civilizations, expands toward the outer solar system, and ultimately reaches the Oort Cloud.

Use the three attached images as the primary visual references:

* **Reference Image 1:** Main gameplay art direction. Use its dark navy illustrated star field, flowing cloud-like space formations, scattered stars, subtle colored celestial objects, and calm hand-painted appearance.
* **Reference Image 2:** Start screen environment. Use its futuristic observation room, large central window, blue and violet interior lighting, restrained orange highlights, detailed machinery, and cinematic view of a planet.
* **Reference Image 3:** Endgame visual. Use its immense spherical Oort Cloud surrounding a small illuminated solar system to communicate scale, completion, and transcendence.

Do not copy the images exactly. Create an original design that follows their atmosphere, composition, color balance, and illustrated science-fiction style.

## Core Art Direction

The design should feel:

* Mature
* Peaceful
* Mysterious
* Scientific
* Cinematic
* Expansive
* Premium
* Slightly melancholic
* Visually rewarding

Avoid:

* Cartoon mobile-game graphics
* Bright casino-style colors
* Excessive neon
* Childish icons
* Oversized bubbly buttons
* Dense spreadsheet-like dashboards
* Generic glassmorphism covering the entire screen
* Realistic NASA photography

Use a polished 2D illustrated style with hand-painted textures, simplified celestial forms, soft gradients, subtle grain, restrained glow, and layered depth.

## Color Palette

Use a dark illustrated cosmic palette:

* Deep space navy: `#071326`
* Midnight blue: `#0C1C36`
* Layered cloud blue: `#122747`
* Panel blue: `#14233D`
* Muted indigo: `#2D3F70`
* Soft violet: `#736AAE`
* Stellar cream: `#FFF1C7`
* Warm orange accent: `#F28A5B`
* Pale planetary blue: `#A9C7DF`
* Life green: `#6EA98B`
* Primary text: `#F4F3EF`
* Secondary text: `#9EACC1`
* Disabled text: `#59677D`

Orange should be used sparingly for primary interactions, active machinery, important alerts, and major progression moments.

## Typography

Use a refined modern sans-serif typeface such as:

* Space Grotesk
* Manrope
* Inter

Use uppercase labels selectively for technical information.

Typography should feel clean and restrained rather than highly futuristic.

Use:

* Large cinematic title text
* Medium-weight section headings
* Compact numerical resource displays
* Small technical labels
* Comfortable paragraph spacing
